# Track B setting options, and the default pick

Status: decision support, 2026-11-01. You said you were not sure how to answer Q-004. This page makes the choice concrete so you can react by gut, and it records a default so the build is not blocked. **Default: Option 1, Sonoran myth, working title "Dry River."** Veto or switch any time before the public web alpha (end of M2); the setting lives in content data and strings, so a switch during M0 or M1 costs days, not weeks.

How to read each option: the one-line pitch, what the first screen feels like, the skill list with names, sample items and monsters, the prestige idea, and the honest risk.

---

## Option 1: Sonoran myth (default), working title "Dry River"

**Pitch.** A frontier of canyons, mesas, dry rivers and ruined missions under two moons. You are a drifter who sets up camp at a dead river and slowly brings a settlement back, trading with caravans, prospecting the hills and holding off the things that come out of the heat.

**First screen.** An ochre and dusk-violet palette, a campfire panel, and a skill list down the left. Numbers tick as you gather. The monsoon bar at the top is the season clock.

**Skills (14).** Gathering: Prospecting (ores and gems from washes and mines), Harvesting (agave, prickly pear, mesquite pods), Woodcutting (mesquite, ironwood, cottonwood), Fishing (riverbed pools and tanks after rains), Trapping (jackrabbit, javelina, quail). Crafting: Smelting, Silversmithing (jewelry and charms), Leatherwork (armor and packs), Masonry (adobe buildings that unlock camp upgrades), Distilling (spirits and tonics), Cooking. Combat: Attack, Defense, Marksmanship. Support: Cartography (unlocks areas and reduces travel time).

**Sample items.** Mesquite log, ironwood plank, turquoise, fire agate, copper ingot, silver concho, rattler-hide vest, caliche brick, agave spirit, saguaro-fruit jam, quail egg, lantern oil, a bandolier, a surveyor's compass, a mission bell.

**Sample monsters.** Dust wraith, chupacabra, javelina boar, lightning hound, mine ghost, caliche golem, the Thunderbird (raid-tier), the Saltwalker (dungeon boss in a dry lakebed).

**Areas.** Dry River Camp, Agave Flats, Copper Wash, the Old Mission, Thunderbird Mesa, Salt Lake Bed, the Deep Mine, Two Moons Pass.

**Prestige: Monsoon.** When the monsoon bar fills you can call the rains: the land resets, but "watermarks" persist (permanent bonuses), and some areas only exist after a flood.

**Why it is the default.** Distinctive in a genre full of generic fantasy; a palette and iconography that read clearly at small icon sizes; authentic to where you live, which is a devlog and press angle; zero third-party IP risk; folklore in the public domain.

**Risk.** Some players may see "western" and expect guns and outlaws; the pitch and art should lean mythic, not cowboy.

## Option 2: Abyss expedition, working title "Pressure"

**Pitch.** A drowned world. From a creaking dive station you salvage the old cities below, farm kelp forests, and build pressure suits to descend one layer deeper.

**First screen.** Deep teal and bioluminescent accents, a depth gauge instead of a season clock, bubbles as the ambient animation.

**Skills.** Salvage, Kelp farming, Shell diving, Net fishing; Welding, Glasswork, Suit crafting, Chemistry (air mixes), Cooking; Harpooning, Shielding, Navigation; Cartography of the trenches.

**Sample items and monsters.** Pressure crystal, brass fitting, lamp fish oil, kelp rope, a rebreather; anglerfish swarm, drowned sentinel, moray lord, the Leviathan.

**Prestige: Descend.** Each layer deeper resets the station but keeps the engineering knowledge.

**Why consider it.** A strong hook and few idle games are deep-sea. **Risk.** Blue-on-blue art is hard to read at icon size; "layers" can feel like the same loop repeated.

---

## Option 3: The archive, working title "Folio"

**Pitch.** Scholars return to a ruined academy and reclaim its knowledge. Excavate, transcribe, bind, enchant; fend off constructs and curses left behind.

**Skills.** Excavation, Transcription, Herbalism, Glasswork; Alchemy, Bookbinding, Enchanting, Cartography; Warding, Invocation, Duel.

**Prestige: New edition.** Republish the archive with corrections; permanent "footnotes."

**Why consider it.** Fits the Magic Research audience, who like systems-dense games. **Risk.** Abstract theme is harder to market with a single screenshot; closer to existing games.

---

## Option 4: Plain original fantasy

Elves, dwarves, forests, mines. The safest to design and the most crowded shelf; the research counted 54 idle releases in one week, most of them this. Not recommended unless you dislike all three above.

---

## What happens next with the default

M0 ports your existing systems under the Dry River names: skills, items, monsters and areas get new ids and strings; formulas and loops carry over unchanged. A forbidden-terms lint keeps every Blizzard name out of the content and the repo. The final product name gets a trademark search before the Steam page; "Dry River" is a codename until then.
