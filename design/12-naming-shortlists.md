# Naming: process and shortlists

Status: design, 2026-11-01. Nothing here has been checked for availability. Names are candidates for your reaction, not recommendations to use.

## Process (runs once per product, before anything public)

1. Shortlist five names from the lists below plus any of yours.
2. Checks per name: USPTO trademark search in the relevant classes (software and data services for Track A; games and downloadable software for Track B); Steam store search (Track B); exact-match domain availability for `.com` and one alternative; social handles; a web search for products with the same name in the same space; pronunciation and spelling test (say it to someone, have them type it).
3. Record results in `DECISIONS.md` and pick. A codename stays in use until this is done.

## Track A candidates (data product; neutral, descriptive, trustworthy)

| Candidate | Why | Risk to check |
|---|---|---|
| Boardline | Licensing boards, a line you can call | Generic word combinations may be taken |
| Licensewire | Says what it is: a wire of license events | Descriptive names are weak trademarks |
| Registry Signal | Public registries, change signals | Two words, longer domain |
| Statusfeed | The change feed is the product | Generic |
| Permitfold | If the vertical becomes permits | Vertical-specific |
| Civic Index | Broad enough for adjacent record sets | Common phrase |
| Ledgerwatch | Watching public ledgers | "Ledger" reads as accounting |
| Credentia | Credentials, verification | Possible conflicts in identity space |
| Northboard | Neutral, brandable | Means nothing yet |
| Verifact | Verification plus facts | Likely crowded |

Default codename until a decision: `records-platform`.
