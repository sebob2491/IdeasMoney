# IdeasMoney

Research and recommendations for what one person can build with an AI coding agent in 2026 that has real monetary value, and how to run that build mostly hands-off.

- **Start here:** [`WHAT-TO-BUILD.md`](WHAT-TO-BUILD.md). Executive summary, ranked ideas with scores, advanced build-and-test methods, a hands-off pipeline design for this repo, tailwinds with dates, reverse engineering and browser-remake questions, and the decisions needed to begin.
- **Designs:** [`design/`](design/) holds the build designs: the shared hands-off pipeline, Track A (public-records data product) and Track B (original-IP idle game).
- **Decisions:** [`DECISIONS.md`](DECISIONS.md) is the running decision log and the open questions to answer each week.
- **Evidence:** [`research/`](research/) holds the seven underlying research reports with roughly 700 cited URLs. Figures are tagged verified, self-reported, estimate or unverified.

Status: research and designs complete as of 2026-11-01; Tracks A and B approved to proceed in parallel; nothing has been built yet. Next step is answering the open questions in `DECISIONS.md`, then the setup week in `design/00-studio-pipeline.md` Section 12.
