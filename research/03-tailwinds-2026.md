# Tailwinds 2026–2027: where a one-person + Claude Code build can capture real value

**Prepared:** 2026-11-01 for a solo builder in Arizona (direction-only involvement, months-long autonomous build).
**Method:** ~50 web searches and ~60 page fetches, prioritising primary sources (Federal Register, ada.gov, FinCEN, Census Bureau, azleg.gov, city ordinances, Steamworks docs, vendor pricing pages, court-ruling coverage). Every dated claim carries a URL. The session's search budget ran out before a few items could be checked; they are listed in §12, not guessed.

**Confidence legend:** **[P]** primary (government, court, official docs, vendor's own pricing page) · **[S]** reputable secondary (law firm, trade press, FPF, Sacra) · **[V]** vendor/marketing/SEO source, directional only · **[U]** unverified or conflicting.

## 1. Executive summary

1. **The biggest datable US software-demand event of 2027–2028 is the ADA Title II web rule.** DOJ's April 20, 2026 interim final rule moved compliance to **April 26, 2027** (entities serving ≥50,000) and **April 26, 2028** (smaller entities, special districts), keeping WCAG 2.1 AA. The National Federation of the Blind is suing to undo the extension (summary-judgment motion Sept 28, 2026). The covered universe is **91,438 local governments** including **12,535 independent school districts** and **40,199 special districts** — mostly tiny bodies with no accessibility staff. Private ADA web suits reached **3,117 federal filings in 2025**, and ~25% of September-2026 defendants were running an accessibility overlay when sued — an indictment of the incumbent "install a widget" category.
2. **Most other 2026 "deadlines" slipped, narrowed, or died**: EU AI Act high-risk duties moved to Dec 2, 2027 / Aug 2, 2028; Colorado's AI Act was repealed and replaced (SB 26-189, effective Jan 1, 2027, AG-only, under a federal stay); the Corporate Transparency Act now **permanently exempts all US companies** (final rule Aug 11, 2026). Do not build for these.
3. **California's Delete Act DROP is the privacy deadline that actually bit**: brokers must process deletion lists every 45 days from Aug 1, 2026, at $200 per request per day in penalties, with audits from Jan 1, 2028. One secondary report says only ~30% of brokers were processing in week one against ~450,000 requests [U].
4. **Agent infrastructure is a distribution channel, not a revenue model.** Anthropic's directory is open to any paid plan with no revenue share and bans ads and autonomous money movement; OpenAI's Apps SDK allows commerce for physical goods only and bars in-app digital subscriptions; Instant Checkout was reportedly retired in March 2026. The "<5% of MCP servers are monetized" statistic was retracted by its publisher.
5. **Where small vertical software has demonstrably won:** ZenMaid (bootstrapped maid-service scheduling, ~$2.6–3M/yr) and Shovels (permit-data API, $6K ARR Aug 2023 → ~$3M ARR Aug 2026 per Sacra, from only ~2,770 of 20,000+ permitting jurisdictions). Incumbent pricing leaves room: ServiceTitan targets 20+ technicians; AppFolio has a 50-unit minimum; Skimmer doubles its per-location price at 50 pools.
6. **Scraping law is bifurcating:** public data on private platforms is still not a CFAA crime, but terms-of-service contract and interference claims now **survive dismissal** (Reddit v. Anthropic, Sept 19, 2026; Reddit v. Perplexity, July 31, 2026), and Cloudflare defaults to blocking/charging crawlers. Government public records are the legally cleanest substrate for a data product.
7. **Idle games are real but saturating:** Rusty's Retirement sold 550k+ copies solo; Nodebuster ($2.99, solo) has 10,703 Steam reviews at 97%; Melvor Idle landed a Jagex deal. But "Idler" releases went 329 (2023) → 940 (2025) → 1,566 in Jan–Sep 2026 (+150%). A WoW-asset fan game is a near-certain takedown.

## 3. Regulatory deadlines in detail

### 3.1 ADA Title II — the anchor tailwind
DOJ's interim final rule (published and effective **April 20, 2026**, 91 FR 20902, comments closed June 22) delays compliance by one year: ≥50,000-population entities to **April 26, 2027**, smaller entities and special districts to **April 26, 2028**. WCAG 2.1 AA and the five exceptions (archived content, pre-existing documents, third-party content, individualized password-protected documents, pre-existing social media) are unchanged; ada.gov names public schools, community colleges and universities explicitly. [Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/api/v1/documents/2026-07663.json) [P]; [ada.gov](https://www.ada.gov/resources/2024-03-08-web-rule/) [P]. The NFB challenged the DOJ and parallel HHS §504 extensions; one tracker dates the filing to May 27, 2026 and reports a summary-judgment motion on **Sept 28, 2026**, with no final rule as of Oct 28 — a win would pull dates forward. [titleii.org](https://titleii.org/title-ii-updates) [S]; [Seyfarth](https://www.adatitleiii.com/2026/04/doj-extends-ada-title-ii-website-accessibility-deadlines-for-governmental-entities-but-litigation-and-compliance-risks-remain/) [S]. DOJ itself cited "significant litigation risks" for entities missing the original dates, since Title II carries a private right of action with fee-shifting.

The 2025 Census of Governments counts **91,438** local governments: 3,031 counties, 19,489 municipalities, 16,184 townships, **12,535 independent school districts**, **40,199 special districts** (plus 1,318 dependent school systems covered through their parent governments). [Census](https://cdn.www.census.gov/content/dam/Census/library/publications/2025/econ/govtorg2225.pdf) [P]. The 2028 cohort is therefore the vast majority of entities and the least staffed. On the private side, Seyfarth counted **3,117** federal website suits in 2025 (2,452 in 2024); UsableNet's fed+state tracker counted 4,928 and projects ≈6,176 for 2026; in September 2026, **91 of 360 sued companies (≈25%) were running an accessibility widget**. [accessibility.build](https://accessibility.build/research/accessibility-lawsuits) [S]. No public dataset of small-entity readiness was found; law-firm and vendor guidance [S/V] converges on inventories, PDF remediation and procurement-language changes.

**Gap:** a WCAG 2.1 AA monitor → remediation-workflow → PDF/DOCX remediation → exception register → attestation product priced for a town of 8,000 or a 1,500-student district, sold through school-board associations, councils of governments and cooperative purchasing contracts (which bypass RFPs). Slow sales motion, but the deadline is hard, countable and two years out.

### 3.2 European Accessibility Act
Directive 2019/882 covers e-commerce, banking, e-books, telecom, audiovisual, transport and consumer hardware; obligations apply since **June 28, 2025**. [EC](https://commission.europa.eu/strategy-and-policy/policies/justice-and-fundamental-rights/disability/union-equality-strategy-rights-persons-disabilities-2021-2030/european-accessibility-act_en) [P]; [Foster](https://www.foster.com/newsroom/legal-alerts/us-businesses-must-prepare-for-eu-accessibility-act-compliance-by-june-28/) [S]. Enforcement is complaint-driven; claims of investigations of US brands come only from vendors [V]. Treat as a reason ADA buyers also want an EN 301 549 report, not a standalone market.

### 3.3 EU AI Act and US state AI laws
The Digital Omnibus on AI (Parliament June 16, Council June 29, in force **July 27, 2026**) moved Annex III high-risk duties from Aug 2, 2026 to **Dec 2, 2027** and Annex I to **Aug 2, 2028**; Article 50 transparency still applies **Aug 2, 2026** (transitional Dec 2, 2026 for some existing systems). [FPF](https://fpf.org/blog/the-ai-act-implementation-timeline-what-changes-under-the-ai-omnibus/) [S]; [Gibson Dunn](https://www.gibsondunn.com/eu-ai-act-omnibus-agreement-postponed-high-risk-deadlines-and-other-key-changes/) [S]. Colorado's SB 24-205 was delayed to June 30, 2026, stayed by a federal court (Apr 27, 2026), then **repealed and replaced by SB 26-189 (signed May 14, 2026; effective Jan 1, 2027)**, which drops the duty of care, risk programs and impact assessments, keeps developer disclosures, adds consumer notices, 30-day adverse-decision disclosures and 3-year record retention, and is AG-only with a 60-day cure until 2030. [Eckert Seamans](https://www.eckertseamans.com/legal-updates/colorado-repeals-and-replaces-its-landmark-ai-statute-what-businesses-need-to-know) [S]. Texas TRAIGA took effect Jan 1, 2026; California ADMT notice/opt-out rights start Jan 1, 2027 with risk assessments due April 1, 2028. [MultiState](https://www.multistate.us/insider/2026/2/4/all-of-the-comprehensive-privacy-laws-that-take-effect-in-2026) [S]. **Verdict:** small companies will not pay a solo vendor for AI-Act tooling in 2027; Elad Gil lists "compliance" as still open, but at enterprise scale.

### 3.4 Privacy and data brokers
Twenty states have comprehensive privacy laws; 2026 brought Indiana, Kentucky and Rhode Island (Jan 1), Oregon and Nebraska amendments (Jan 1), Connecticut/Arkansas/Utah (Jul 1) and California's SB 361 broker amendments (Aug 1). [MultiState](https://www.multistate.us/insider/2026/2/4/all-of-the-comprehensive-privacy-laws-that-take-effect-in-2026) [S]. Consent management is saturated. **The exception is California's Delete Act.** From **Aug 1, 2026** every registered broker must pull the DROP deletion list at least every **45 days**, hash/match, delete (including inferences) and report; penalties are **$200 per request per day** with no cure; audits begin **Jan 1, 2028**. [CPPA](https://privacy.ca.gov/drop-for-data-brokers/) [P]; [OneTrust](https://www.onetrust.com/blog/californias-drop-what-the-delete-act-changes-for-consent-preferences-and-data-deletion/) [V]. A single secondary report says ≈30% of brokers were processing in week one against ≈450,000 requests [U]. Enterprise privacy platforms serve this; a cheap, auditable DROP processor for small brokers is a deadline-driven niche — with real data-processor liability.

### 3.5 E-invoicing and entity reporting
EU: Belgium B2B via Peppol from **Jan 1, 2026**; Poland Feb 1 / Apr 1, 2026; France **Sep 1, 2026** (all receive; large/mid issue), SMEs **Sep 1, 2027**; Germany issue **Jan 2027** (>€800k) and **Jan 2028** (all); ViDA cross-border ≈2030. [Fonoa](https://fonoa.com/blog/e-invoicing-and-digital-reporting-in-the-eu-upcoming-changes) [S]. The SME waves are real demand but buyers are European and Peppol access points need certification; a US solo builder's angle is a connector from Stripe/QuickBooks to a certified access point. US: no mandate, voluntary DBNAlliance network. [Fed](https://fedpaymentsimprovement.org/strategic-initiatives/payments-efficiency/e-invoicing/) [P]. CTA: FinCEN's final rule of **Aug 11, 2026 (effective Aug 14)** permanently exempts US companies and persons. [FinCEN](https://www.fincen.gov/boi) [P]. 1099 changes were not researched (§12).

### 3.6 Arizona
- **STRs.** Scottsdale (Ordinance 4566) requires a per-property licence for stays under 30 days: **$250/year**, **$500,000 liability insurance**, neighbour notification within 30 days (licence number, address, 24-hour contact) and on changes; Ordinance 4719 bans event venues. [Scottsdale](https://www.scottsdaleaz.gov/codes-and-ordinances/vacation-and-short-term-rentals) [P]; [owner requirements](https://www.scottsdaleaz.gov/codes-and-ordinances/vacation-and-short-term-rentals/information-for-owners-and-operators) [P]. Sedona has required a per-unit permit since July 6, 2022; Ordinance 2025-02 (Mar 25, 2025) added an entrance posting; guides report a $210 fee and outright denial of incomplete applications since Oct 23, 2025 [V]. Phoenix requires registration (reported $250; May 2026 amendment [U] — official page not reachable). All operators need a TPT licence. [Sedona guide](https://gotravli.com/sedona-short-term-rental-rules-permits-2026/) [V]; [Hostaway](https://www.hostaway.com/blog/airbnb-rules-in-arizona/) [V]. ## 6. Data/API products from continuous scraping — demand and legality

**Demand.** Shovels (founded 2022) grew from ≈$6K ARR (Aug 2023) to $500K (end 2024), $1.5M (end 2025) and ≈$3M ARR (Aug 2026, Sacra estimates), raised $6.5M, prices at Free/$599/$999 per month, and covers 178M permits across ≈2,770 of **20,000+ permitting jurisdictions**; buyers are lead-gen, contractor-intelligence, telecom, homebuilders, lenders and utilities; competitors are BuildZoom, Cotality, ATTOM. [Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/shovels/) [S]. Rewiring America runs a foundation-backed incentives API/calculator. [Rewiring America](https://www.rewiringamerica.org/api) [P]. Court-records vendors were not checked (§12).

**Legality (US, Nov 2026).**
- *CFAA:* hiQ v. LinkedIn — the Ninth Circuit held (reaffirmed Apr 2022) that scraping public data is likely not "without authorization"; the Dec 2022 settlement imposed a permanent injunction on hiQ and a stipulation that LinkedIn "may establish liability," so it has no precedential value. [Proskauer](https://www.proskauer.com/blog/hiq-and-linkedin-reach-proposed-settlement-in-landmark-scraping-case) [S]; [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HiQ_Labs_v._LinkedIn) [S].
- *Logged-out ToS:* Meta v. Bright Data (Jan 2024) — no breach; Meta dropped the suit. X Corp v. Bright Data (May 2024, Alsup) — dismissed; X's theory would create "de facto copyright ownership." [MediaPost](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/392920/judge-sides-against-meta-in-battle-over-scraping-p.html) [S]; [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2024/05/10/elon-musks-x-loses-lawsuit-against-bright-data-over-data-scraping.html) [S]; [NatLawReview](https://natlawreview.com/article/x-corp-loses-battle-over-public-data-access) [S].
- *2026 turn:* **Reddit v. Anthropic** (N.D. Cal. No. 3:25-cv-05643, **Sept 19, 2026**) — breach of contract, intentional interference and unfair competition survived and were held not preempted because each "required an extra element beyond unauthorized copying." [DPSI](https://www.dataprivacyandsecurityinsider.com/2026/09/terms-of-service-just-got-teeth-reddits-scraping-suit-against-anthropic-survives/) [S]; [Bloomberg Law](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/ip-law/anthropic-loses-bid-to-dismiss-reddit-ai-scraping-privacy-suit) [S]. **Reddit v. Perplexity/SerpApi/Oxylabs/AWMProxy** (S.D.N.Y., Engelmayer, **July 31, 2026**) — DMCA anti-circumvention and conspiracy claims proceed. [MediaPost](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/416950/reddit-can-proceed-with-scraping-claims-against-pe.html) [S]; [Law360](https://www.law360.com/articles/2508228/reddit-ai-data-scraping-case-mostly-survives-dismissal) [S]. Neither decides liability, but a platform with drafted terms and a cease-and-desist letter now gets past dismissal.
- *Training copyright:* Bartz v. Anthropic (June 23, 2025) — training on lawfully acquired books was fair use, the pirated library was not; **$1.5B settlement** (Sept 2025, approved July 2026). [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bartz_v._Anthropic) [S].
- *Gatekeeping:* Cloudflare pay-per-crawl launched July 1, 2025 (private beta, HTTP 402, Cloudflare as merchant of record) [Cloudflare](https://blog.cloudflare.com/introducing-pay-per-crawl/) [P]; default blocking of mixed-use crawlers from Sept 15, 2026 is secondary-only [U]. RSL adds a `License:` line to robots.txt with Reddit, Yahoo, Cloudflare, Fastly, Akamai, O'Reilly, Vox and Ziff Davis as supporters but names no paying AI company. [rslstandard.org](https://rslstandard.org/) [P].

**Implication.** Scraping consumer platforms for a commercial dataset is now litigation-exposed even for public data. **Government public records — permits, licences, STR permits, zoning decisions, ordinances, dockets — carry none of the ToS-contract exposure the 2026 rulings turned on**, and Shovels proves buyers pay once records are normalised across jurisdictions. The asset is the normalisation layer and update cadence (still respect robots.txt and vendor-hosted portal terms).

## 9. Where value accrues — five analyses

1. **a16z, Immerman & Rodriguez (Mar 2, 2026):** "code has never been where the value lies"; via Helmer's Seven Powers, switching costs weaken, while network effects, scale, brand and cornered resources (proprietary data) strengthen; **process power** — software as an organisation's "stored process" — is strongest; counter-positioning via new pricing wins. [a16z](https://a16z.com/good-news-ai-will-eat-application-software/) [S].
2. **Ben Thompson, "Agents Over Bubbles" (Mar 16, 2026):** modular layers commoditise, integrated layers profit; "Protections exist but they have to do with integration and distribution." [Stratechery](https://stratechery.com/2026/agents-over-bubbles/) [S].
3. **Ben Thompson, "Apps, Agents, and Aggregation" (Sep 28, 2026):** apps become "implementation details"; the scarce resource shifts to "volition"; "agents operate better the more context they have about you." Be the system that holds the context, not the UI. [Stratechery](https://stratechery.com/2026/apps-agents-and-aggregation/) [S].
4. **Elad Gil, "AI Market Clarity" (Jul 22, 2025):** first-wave segments are locked in; **accounting, compliance, financial tools, sales tooling and security remain open**; pricing shifts from seats to labor equivalents; distribution plus technology wins. [Elad Gil](https://blog.eladgil.com/p/ai-market-clarity) [S].
5. **Paul Graham (Feb 10, 2025):** "If AI makes writing code more of a commodity, understanding users' problems will become the most important component of starting a startup." [X](https://x.com/paulg/status/1888912942139113496) [P]. Sacra's wrapper research is the empirical companion. [Sacra](https://sacra.com/research/copy-ai-jasper-ai-writing-tools) [S].

**Synthesis:** durable solo value in 2027 = (a) proprietary, continuously refreshed, legally clean data; (b) embedded compliance evidence a buyer must keep for years; (c) distribution through a trade or government channel incumbents ignore; (d) usage pricing that counter-positions per-seat minimums. Code is the cheapest part of all four.
